Built on Research, Not Guesswork

HusÖga uses published methods from:

* Building condition assessment
* Building pathology
* Lifecycle deterioration modelling
* Moisture and mold risk research
* Residential maintenance forecasting

The model combines component age, expected service life, construction era, foundation type, ventilation type, and known Swedish housing risk patterns into a simple house risk snapshot.

The methodology is informed by Swedish and Nordic sources on:

* Technical lifespans for roofs, drainage, wet rooms, plumbing, heating systems, and installations
* Moisture, mold, and ventilation risks in residential buildings
* Known high-risk house types such as crawlspaces, slab foundations, aging drainage systems, and older wet rooms
* Indoor environment risks linked to poor ventilation and moisture damage

Sources include:

* Boverket guidance on ventilation, moisture, and indoor environment
* Folkhälsomyndigheten guidance on dampness, mold, and indoor health
* Swedish service-life references from SBR and insurance companies
* Nordic research on building deterioration and moisture-sensitive construction
