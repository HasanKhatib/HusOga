Built on Research, Not Guesswork

HusÖga is built using published research on:

* Building deterioration
* Moisture and mold risk
* Technical lifespan of roofs, drainage, wet rooms, heating systems, and ventilation
* Swedish housing risk patterns

The app combines component age, construction era, foundation type, and expected service life into a simple risk snapshot for house buyers.

Inspired by Swedish guidance from Boverket, Folkhälsomyndigheten, SBR, and insurance industry maintenance references.
