How HusÖga Works

HusÖga estimates house risk using:

* Age of major building components
* Expected technical lifespan
* Construction era
* Foundation type
* Ventilation type
* Known Swedish moisture and maintenance risks

The model is informed by Swedish guidance on ventilation, moisture, indoor environment, drainage, wet rooms, and building lifespans.

HusÖga does not replace a professional inspection. It is designed as an early-stage risk screening tool before bidding or purchase.
